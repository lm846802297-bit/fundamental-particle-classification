# Dimension-by-Dimension Research

The project proceeds strictly in increasing spacetime dimension. A dimension is not closed merely because a familiar theorem has been quoted: its particle criterion, localization assumptions, statistics structure, candidate constructions, and no-go results must be compared before moving upward.

| Order | Spacetime | Status | Gate before moving on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1+1 | **Active** | Resolve conformal braid sectors, massive kink one-particle states and quasi-statistics, factorized-scattering exchange algebras, and deformed-spacetime candidates |
| 2 | 2+1 | Known positive benchmark; detailed review deferred | Re-derive the relativistic anyon/plekton construction and its localization-dependent spin-statistics theorem |
| 3 | 3+1 | Pending | Establish the exact DHR/BF and infinite-spin constraints and inspect assumptions outside their scope |
| 4 | 4+1 | Pending | Derive the relevant little groups and prove which sector/statistics theorems extend |
| 5+ | Higher dimensions | Pending, sequential | Repeat the same dimension-specific analysis; do not extrapolate a theorem without checking its hypotheses |

The project starts at 1+1 because it is the lowest nontrivial relativistic spacetime dimension. 0+1 quantum mechanics has no spatial particle-exchange configuration space and is not treated as a separate relativistic-QFT stage.

## Dimension closure rule

For each dimension, record:

1. the physical one-particle and asymptotic-particle criteria;
2. the relevant spacetime-symmetry representations;
3. the topology of identical-particle configuration space;
4. exchange statistics and superselection-sector composition;
5. point, bounded-region, wedge, string, or cone localization;
6. explicit relativistic QFT constructions and their assumptions;
7. no-go theorems and their exact hypotheses;
8. whether any candidate is physically inequivalent to ordinary Bose/Fermi particles and is genuinely a fundamental particle.

Only after these items are sufficiently resolved do we advance to the next spacetime dimension. Known results in later dimensions remain recorded as background benchmarks, not as a reason to skip the current stage.
